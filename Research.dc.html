Research

Peer-reviewed aerothermal methods, and open questions in surrogate modeling.

Two published methods for qualifying avionics thermal design, three funded research programmes, and an ongoing interest in making expensive CFD and FSI campaigns cheap enough to query in a design loop.

Publications

Peer-reviewed work

2024
ICASET 2023 · Springer, Singapore

A Minimal Input Dimensionless Empirical-Corrector (MIDEC) Method for Aerothermal Analysis of Airborne Electronics

Proceedings of the First International Conference on Aeronautical Sciences, Engineering and Technology. Introduces a dimensionless empirical-corrector method that reduces the input burden of aerothermal LRU analysis while retaining accuracy across the flight envelope, aimed at qualification workflows where full CFD sweeps for every altitude/Mach/duty-cycle combination are impractical.

View on Springer (DOI)
2022
IBCAST 2022 · IEEE

A Figure of Merit Based Computational Heat Transfer Method for Aerothermal Analysis of Airborne Electronics System LRU

19th International Bhurban Conference on Applied Sciences and Technology (IBCAST), Islamabad, pp. 70–75. Proposes a figure-of-merit based computational heat transfer method for evaluating avionics LRU cooling designs, developed and validated against AEROFLUX programme test data.

Current interests

Current interests

Open to collaboration, co-authorship, or a PhD and research-assistant conversation in any of these directions.

PINNs

Physics-informed neural networks

Embedding governing PDEs directly into network training for aerothermal and aerodynamic problems, to cut solver dependence without losing physical fidelity.

ROMs

Reduced-order modeling

Building fast surrogates from high-fidelity CFD and FSI datasets using proper orthogonal decomposition and related methods, for real-time or many-query design work.

Digital twins

UAV digital twin frameworks

Coupling telemetry, low-fidelity physics and simulation-informed correction into a live model of an aircraft in operation. There is a working build of this under Projects.

HPC

High-speed and hypersonic computational fluid dynamics

Compressible, shock-capturing CFD for transonic and supersonic flows, where standard incompressible solvers and mesh strategies stop being reliable.
