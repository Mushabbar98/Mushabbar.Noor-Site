01 / WORK

Selected work

A handful of programs where simulation had to survive contact with real hardware, test chambers and certification standards.

Avionics thermal qualification

AEROFLUX I–III

Air University · Shocks & Stars
2020 – Present
Challenge

Airborne electronics (LRUs) must survive extreme thermal envelopes across altitude, Mach and duty cycle — and be certified to MIL-STD-5400.

Solution

Analytical and CFD aerothermal models across the full flight envelope, a response-surface DoE method, and a MATLAB tool predicting temperature over the whole regime.

Tools
ANSYS FluentMATLABDoE / RSM
Results

Qualified to MIL-STD-5400 for airworthiness certification · validated to within [X]°C of environmental-chamber tests · 3 funded research grants.

UAV aerodynamics & aeroelasticity

Unmanned Platforms

Baykar Technologies
2024 – Present
Challenge

Unmanned platforms need verified aerodynamic performance and flutter-free structural limits before they ever fly.

Solution

CFD across UAV and propeller configurations, FSI-based aeroelastic analysis for flutter boundaries, VN-diagram and flight-envelope definition, plus modal and G-load assessment.

Tools

Hypersonics · configuration trade study

Scramjet Aerothermodynamics

Shocks & Stars Engineering
2022 – 2024
Challenge

Hypersonic propulsion demands a grip on aerothermal loads and combustion behaviour at extreme Mach — and a defensible choice between competing configurations.

Solution

Computational hypersonic aerothermodynamic and combustion analysis across candidate scramjet configurations, scored on a defined figure of merit to select the strongest design.

05 / ABOUT

About

I'm a mechanical engineer who works where fluids, heat and structure meet — the places most likely to decide whether hardware flies or fails.

Over four years I've moved from a university research lab into two engineering companies, carrying the same thread throughout: build a high-fidelity model, make it survive real test data, and turn it into a decision someone can certify or ship. That's meant qualifying avionics to military standards, defining flight envelopes for unmanned aircraft, and analysing combustion inside a scramjet.

I think in systems rather than single simulations, and I'm most useful when a problem sits between disciplines. I'm now focused on the next shift in the field — machine-learning-accelerated simulation — so the pipelines I build stay ahead of the work, not behind it.
