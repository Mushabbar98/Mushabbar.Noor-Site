Industrial & independent projects

Five case studies, from a certified UAV programme to an independently built turbojet.

Every case study below follows the same structure: the problem, the method, the tools, and the numbers — so you can judge the work on its substance.

UAVCFDFSI

UAV Aerodynamics & Aeroelastic Flutter

CFD-based aerodynamic optimization, VN-diagrams and flight envelope definition, and 2-way FSI flutter analysis for Baykar UAV airframes and propeller engines.

Baykar Technologies · 2024–present
TurbomachineryCFDStructural

80 N Small-Scale Turbojet

Independent design of a centrifugal-compressor turbojet — thermodynamic cycle, meanline design, blade geometry, meshing, CFD and modal/aeroelastic structural validation.

Independent project · Vista TF, BladeGen, TurboGrid, CFX
AutomotiveCFDOptimization

Ground Vehicle Aerodynamic Benchmark

High-speed CFD benchmark of a streamlined bluff body in ground proximity, validated against DrivAer and Morelli minimum-drag references, plus adjoint-based shape optimization.

Independent study · Ansys Fluent, k-ω SST
UAVDigital twinPython

2 kg Acrobatic UAV Digital Twin

A full digital-twin ecosystem: 6-DOF flight simulation, real-time MAVLink telemetry, a low-fidelity physics suite running under 2 ms/cycle, and a live comparison dashboard.

Independent project · MATLAB/Simulink, Python, Three.js
AerothermalMIL-STDCFD

Avionics LRU Thermal Qualification (AEROFLUX)

Three funded programmes qualifying airborne electronics LRUs under MIL-E-5400 across conditioned and unconditioned bays, altitude, Mach and engine bleed settings.

Air University / Shocks & Stars · 2020–present
