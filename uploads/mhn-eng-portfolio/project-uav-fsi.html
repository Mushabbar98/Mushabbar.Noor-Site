UAV Aerodynamics & Aeroelastic Flutter Analysis

CFD-driven aerodynamic optimization, VN-diagram flight-envelope definition, and fluid-structure interaction (FSI) flutter analysis for unmanned aerial vehicle airframes and propeller engines.

Client
Baykar Technologies
Role
Aerodynamics Engineer
Duration
Feb 2024 – present
Tools
Fluent · Mechanical · ICEM

Problem

UAV airframes and their propeller engines need aerodynamic performance and structural safety margins established before flight test — bad assumptions at this stage are expensive and, on a loitering munition platform, safety-critical. Static and dynamic stability, flutter boundaries, and G-load structural limits all have to be defined and then re-validated whenever a design changes.

Approach

I run CFD analysis on the UAV airframe and propeller engines to optimize aerodynamic efficiency, evaluating static and dynamic stability parameters that determine flight control response. In parallel, I conduct fluid-structure interaction (2-way coupled Fluent + Mechanical, visualized in CFD-Post) to identify flutter boundaries and set structural limits, and lead G-load structural assessments whenever a design modification is proposed. Modal analysis on the airframe identifies natural frequencies so resonance risk can be designed out early rather than discovered in test. Aerodynamic parameters feed directly into hardware-in-the-loop (HILS) development, and the flight envelope is formalized as a VN-diagram defining safe operating limits by load factor and airspeed.

Validation

Computational predictions are cross-checked against structural ground testing of the aerostructures, closing the loop between simulation and physical hardware before any result is signed off.
