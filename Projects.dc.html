Industrial & independent projects

Three case studies from AeroFlux Lab, Air University.

Each one is written the same way: the challenge, the method, the tools used, and the numbers that came out.

AerothermalMIL-STDCFD

AEROFLUX III: Controlled Bay

Thermal qualification of high-flux electronics in a controlled bay, cooled by engine bleed air and validated to MIL-E-5400 across the altitude envelope.

AeroFlux Lab, Air University · Ansys Fluent + MATLAB
AerothermalDoECFD

AEROFLUX II: Unconditioned Dorsal Bay

Response-surface thermal qualification of electronics in an unconditioned dorsal bay, validated against environmental-chamber testing within 5% error.

AeroFlux Lab, Air University · Fluent + Design Xplorer
HypersonicsCombustionCFD

Scramjet Combustion Analysis

CFD analysis of a supersonic-combustion ramjet engine for a hypersonic suborbital vehicle, comparing constant-area and variable-area combustion chambers.

AeroFlux Lab, Air University · Ansys Fluent + MATLAB
