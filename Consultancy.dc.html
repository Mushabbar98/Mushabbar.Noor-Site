What I take on

The work I'm usually asked for

CFD

CFD validation studies

External and internal flow simulation, benchmarked against published or test data. Automotive bodies, UAVs and general aerodynamic shapes.

FSI

FSI & aeroelastic flutter checks

2-way coupled fluid-structure interaction to establish flutter boundaries and structural limits for lifting surfaces and slender structures.

Structural

Structural & modal analysis

G-load assessments, modal/resonance checks, and static structural evaluation of aerospace or industrial components.

Aerothermal

Thermal & aerothermal analysis

Electronics and avionics thermal management, heat-exchanger performance, and MIL-STD/MIL-E-5400-style qualification reporting.

Wind energy

Wind turbine FSI

Blade-level aeroelastic and structural response studies for wind energy applications.

Open-source

Open-source CFD validation

Benchmarking and validating open-source CFD workflows (OpenFOAM-class tools) against commercial-solver or experimental baselines.

Engagement models

Three ways to scope the work

Everything starts with a scoping call. Treat these as starting points rather than a fixed menu.

Short consultation

Focused review

A second opinion on an existing CFD/FEA setup, results sanity-check, or a scoped technical question answered with references.

  • 1–2 sessions or async review
  • Written findings memo
  • Good fit for a quick go/no-go
Most common

Focused analysis

One simulation study run start to finish: one geometry, one set of operating conditions, a validated result.

  • Problem definition & setup
  • Meshing, solve, post-processing
  • Benchmarked, documented results
  • 1–3 week typical turnaround
Flagship

Full engagement

Multi-condition studies, design-of-experiment sweeps, or coupled multiphysics work spanning CFD, FSI and thermal.

  • Multiple design points / DoE
  • Coupled multiphysics (FSI, thermal)
  • Full qualification-style report
  • Ongoing advisory available
Process

How an engagement runs

01

Discovery

Scoping call to understand the problem, constraints, and what decision the result needs to support.

02

Scope

Written proposal: method, tools, deliverables, timeline and fixed or capped cost.

03

Analysis

Setup, meshing, solving and post-processing, with progress check-ins on longer engagements.

04

Delivery

Documented results with methodology, benchmarks, and a plain-language summary of findings.

05

Review

A walkthrough call and a revision window to make sure the result actually answers your question.
